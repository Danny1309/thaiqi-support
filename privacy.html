ThaiQI Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE_HERE

Summary

ThaiQI shows air quality information (AQI/PM2.5) for a location you choose (current location or searched location).

Location

If you allow location access, ThaiQI uses your approximate location to request local air quality results. You can deny location permission and use search instead.

Notifications

If you enable alerts, ThaiQI can send local notifications when AQI meets or exceeds your chosen threshold.

Advertising

If ads are enabled, ad partners may collect device information for ad delivery and measurement.
